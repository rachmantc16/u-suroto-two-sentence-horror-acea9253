A two-sentence horror story, written by a small Node program. A night nurse swears she never left a silent mother's room — and the hallway camera backs her up, frame after frame of her standing motionless at the foot of the bed. The second sentence turns the first: the mother who has not spoken in two years now speaks, in the nurse's voice. The horror is never explained; what stood at the bed all night, and what came back wearing the mother's morning, is left for the reader to fill in.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program validates its story before writing it — exactly two sentences, at most 400 characters, not blank — then writes it to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`/output` in the grader, or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` locally).
